Vitabox

Web · UX/UI · Motion

Landing page and onboarding quiz for Vitabox — personalised daily vitamin packs.

(2025)

Prototype

The Vitabox app, start to finish

From how it works to a plan built around you.

  1. Nutrition questionnaire

    3–5 minutes, one question per screen — age, height and health goals, with a progress bar all the way.

  2. Made personal

    Your name appears on a live preview of your daily pack as you type it.

  3. Your plan

    A plan customised for you, ready to add to cart or check out.

Design system

One kit for the app and the web

Typography
Inter — Black for headlines, down to Medium for body copy.
Colour
Brand Yellow leads; Deep Slate, Slate and Cloud support it.
Components
Buttons, goal picker, inputs, cart items, toggles, quiz progress.
Assets
The lemon mascot, daily packs and capsules, as 3D CSS art.

Project summary

A personalised supplement concept: a health assessment sets what your body needs, and a monthly box delivers it in daily packs. I designed the brand identity, logo, UX/UI and visual system — prototype, packaging, mockups, illustrations and promotional visuals.

One pack a day. Personalised for your body. Designed for your routine.
